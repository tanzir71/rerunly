The verification loop

One action.
One fresh look.

The model cannot race ahead on assumptions. Every state-changing action creates new visual evidence before the next decision.

01

Observe

Capture the viewport, page structure, focus, errors, network failures, URL, and tabs.

02

Act once

Choose one fixed browser tool with a stated expected outcome and policy check.

03

Capture

Wait for stability, redact protected regions, and take a new screenshot automatically.

04

Verify or recover

A separate vision pass decides whether to continue, try another strategy, pause, or finish.

Recovery with evidence

It notices when the page says no.

Validation errors, overlays, login walls, blank pages, wrong tabs, redirects, and missing confirmations become explicit recovery signals—not reasons to repeat the same click.

  • At most two corrective attempts per intended step
  • Pause after three no-progress actions
  • Never finish on the model’s claim alone
